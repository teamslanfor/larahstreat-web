Privacy & cookies

This policy explains how Larah Treats, Cakes and Pastries (“we”), Lockwood, Huddersfield, handles information on this website.

Orders and messages

Our order and contact forms don’t store anything on this website. They prepare a message that opens in WhatsApp (or your text messages), and nothing is sent until you press send. We use the details you send only to reply to you and arrange your order.

WhatsApp

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

We don’t use tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded Google Map and Google Fonts may set their own. If you dismiss the cookie notice, your browser remembers that choice locally.

Your rights

To ask about, correct or delete information you’ve sent us, contact us on 07440 678214. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
