Cake FAQs

How much notice do you need?

As much as possible, especially for wedding and tiered cakes. For last-minute orders, message on WhatsApp and Larah will let you know if she can help.

How much does a cake cost?

Prices depend on size, design and flavour. Send your order and Larah will reply with a price before anything is confirmed.

Can you copy a cake from a photo?

Yes! Attach your photo in WhatsApp and Larah will create a design inspired by it.

Do you make Nigerian fruit cake?

Yes, traditional fruit cake is a favourite for weddings and special occasions. Choose “Nigerian fruit cake” as your flavour.

Can you cater for allergies?

Please tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you order, and Larah will let you know what’s possible.
